# E6 — Method review and retained contracts

This inventory closes the method-by-method review requested by handover 06.
Validation of the draw continuation is recorded separately; earlier measurements
remain attached to their original source hashes. No Particle representation or
common extension scheduler is introduced. Rendering internals remain E7 and
full extension lifecycle coverage remains E8.

## Consumer and phase boundaries

Classic mixed-radius collisions, fluid bounds/separation, links/warp, emitters,
absorbers, group/density limits and external interactivity remain covered by
primary integration tests. Fluid clears its instance-local neighbor array before
each synchronous query; other direct consumers retain fresh arrays and CircleWarp
retains its deduplicated shifted-region union. S3 does not justify migrating these
to shared caller buffers.

Updater/fluid movement changes live geometry but does not relocate insertion
cells until the next phase-1 rebuild. A destination-only query can miss a moved
particle; a query including its insertion cell can observe the live position.
This preserved contract is characterized by grid movement fixtures. E6 does not
silently rebuild during fluid iteration or reorder capped neighbors. Reindexing
or a different solver snapshot would require separate trajectories/RNG evidence.

P1–P4 and S1–S4 each have an implemented/retained/rejected decision with data in
IMP-03/04. Synthetic keys, cell reuse and capped pools do not establish complete
scene equivalence. The draw snapshot is a correctness change, not an FPS claim;
its traversal cost and final browser comparison must be reported independently.
